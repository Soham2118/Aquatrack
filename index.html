Investigating greywater in our school cafeteria

A record of our experiments on filtration, water quality and seed growth. This site also includes a calculator to explore possible water savings and a place to log new measurements.

At a glance

Greywater is gently used wastewater from household fixtures that has not come into contact with fecal matter. These are the results from the cafeteria greywater investigation. The experimental result tables below are based on the previously shared readings. The interactive calculator and log are separate tools.
